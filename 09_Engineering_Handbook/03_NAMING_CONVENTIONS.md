# Naming Conventions

- Classes: PascalCase
- Functions/variables: camelCase
- Constants: UPPER_SNAKE_CASE
- Files: kebab-case
- DB tables: snake_case
- Events: Past tense
- Commands: Imperative
- Queries: Question/lookup intent

- `AdvanceWorldTimeCommand`
