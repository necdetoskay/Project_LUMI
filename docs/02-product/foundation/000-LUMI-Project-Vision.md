# Project LUMI

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Project Vision

Project LUMI is not designed as a traditional game.

Its purpose is to create a living, evolving universe where stories emerge naturally from the interactions between people, places, memories, emotions, cultures and time.

The child is not the center of the universe.

Instead, the child enters a world that already exists, already remembers, and continues living even when the application is closed.

Every interaction becomes part of that world's history.

---

# Core Philosophy

The world should never feel scripted.

Stories should not be selected from a database.

Instead, stories emerge naturally because the world itself changes.

A fisherman may lose his boat.

A village may celebrate a festival.

A child may rescue an injured fox.

Years later that fox may return.

Nothing exists because a writer placed it there.

Everything exists because the simulation allowed it to happen.

---

# Living Universe

LUMI is built around one fundamental belief:

> Stories are consequences of life.

Not objectives.

Not quests.

Not missions.

The world lives first.

Stories are only the visible surface of that life.

---

# The Child's Role

The child is a participant.

Not a hero chosen by destiny.

The world does not wait.

Characters continue living.

Villages continue changing.

Animals continue migrating.

Relationships continue evolving.

The child simply becomes part of that ongoing history.

---

# Persistent World

Every meaningful event leaves traces.

Examples include:

- memories
- friendships
- broken promises
- repaired relationships
- planted trees
- abandoned houses
- discovered places
- family histories
- cultural traditions

Nothing important disappears immediately.

The world remembers.

---

# Emotion Before Mechanics

Traditional games reward the player with numbers.

LUMI rewards the player with meaning.

The goal is not:

- more gold
- more XP
- stronger weapons

Instead the child gains:

- memories
- friendships
- trust
- curiosity
- courage
- empathy
- understanding

Progress is emotional rather than numerical.

---

# Emergent Storytelling

Stories are never generated randomly.

They emerge from:

- World State
- Character Goals
- Relationships
- Memories
- Emotions
- Time
- Culture
- Environment
- Chance

Every story is a consequence of previous events.

---

# Long-Term Continuity

The same characters may appear months later.

Animals remember previous encounters.

Families grow.

Children become adults.

Villages change.

Old ruins become forests.

A forgotten promise may eventually become the beginning of a completely different story.

---

# Wonder Instead of Surprise

LUMI does not attempt to constantly surprise the child.

Instead it tries to preserve wonder.

Wonder comes from discovering that the world has continued living independently.

Examples:

- A seed planted long ago has become a tree.
- A rescued dragon now protects a valley.
- A stranger met months ago has become a village leader.
- A forgotten cave has become someone's home.

These moments create emotional continuity rather than scripted plot twists.

---

# Safe Emotional Design

The project is designed for children.

Therefore:

Fear must never become trauma.

Sadness must always leave room for hope.

Conflict should teach understanding.

Failure should create learning.

Every difficult emotion should eventually find a meaningful resolution.

---

# Parent Companion Philosophy

Parents are not spectators.

They are companions.

The system should occasionally help families continue conversations outside the application.

Stories become shared family memories.

---

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence is not used to replace creativity.

AI acts as a silent world gardener.

It observes.

Connects.

Remembers.

Suggests.

But the world itself creates the stories through simulation.

---

# Success Criteria

LUMI succeeds when children say:

"I wonder what happened while I was away."

instead of

"I completed another quest."

Because curiosity is more valuable than completion.
