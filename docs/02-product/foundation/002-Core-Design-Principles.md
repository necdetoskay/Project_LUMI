# Core Design Principles

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

This document defines the non-negotiable principles guiding every system in Project LUMI.

---

# Principle 1 — Simulation First

The world is simulated first.

Stories emerge afterwards.

---

# Principle 2 — No Scripted Heroes

The child is important, but not the center of existence.

---

# Principle 3 — Consequences Over Rewards

Meaningful consequences are preferred over numerical rewards.

---

# Principle 4 — Emotional Continuity

Feelings should evolve naturally.

No instant emotional reversals.

---

# Principle 5 — Hidden Systems

The child should rarely see numerical values.

Trust, fear, friendship and curiosity remain invisible.

---

# Principle 6 — Consistency

Characters behave consistently with their personality, memories and experiences.

---

# Principle 7 — Freedom

Multiple solutions should exist.

There is rarely a single correct answer.

---

# Principle 8 — Long-Term Memory

Actions may affect stories months later.

---

# Principle 9 — Safety

Children should experience challenge without emotional harm.

Hope should always remain possible.

---

# Principle 10 — Discovery

Curiosity is more valuable than completion.

Exploration is more valuable than collecting.
