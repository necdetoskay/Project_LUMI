# Living World Studio

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Living World Studio is the internal content creation environment used to expand Project LUMI.

It allows creators to build new parts of the universe while preserving design consistency.

---

# Studio Functions

The studio may support creation of:

- regions
- cultures
- settlements
- NPC templates
- wildlife
- festivals
- historical events
- story seeds
- landmarks
- traditions

---

# Validation

Before publication, new content should be validated for:

- consistency
- cultural compatibility
- emotional safety
- narrative balance
- simulation integrity

---

# Preview

Creators should be able to preview how new content behaves inside the simulation before release.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may suggest improvements.

Final creative decisions always remain with human designers.

---

# Future Expansion

The studio should support collaborative world building while preserving the identity of the Living Universe.

---

# Design Goal

Provide a safe and scalable foundation for expanding LUMI over many years.
