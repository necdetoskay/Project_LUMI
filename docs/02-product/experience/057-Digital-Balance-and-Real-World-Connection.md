# Digital Balance and Real-World Connection

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

LUMI should strengthen the child's relationship with the real world.

The application complements reality.

It never attempts to replace it.

---

# Healthy Usage

The child should naturally feel comfortable ending a session.

There is no pressure to continue.

Stories patiently wait.

The world remains welcoming.

---

# Real-World Inspiration

Digital experiences may encourage:

- drawing
- reading
- observing nature
- asking questions
- family conversations
- outdoor exploration

The application extends curiosity into everyday life.

---

# Screen Balance

Quiet endings are intentional.

The system avoids encouraging "just one more story."

Natural stopping points are preferred.

---

# Family Participation

Parents may continue stories through conversation.

Shared imagination becomes more valuable than additional screen time.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Children should remember what they experienced, not how long they stayed in the application.

---

# Design Goal

Support healthy digital habits while strengthening real-world curiosity.
