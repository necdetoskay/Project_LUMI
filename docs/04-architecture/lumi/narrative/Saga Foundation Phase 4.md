# Saga Foundation Phase 4

Phase 4 turns the selected Character Genesis into long-horizon narrative foundation state.

## Authority

Saga Foundation owns Core Tension, the immediate/medium/Core Saga time scales, protected Saga Canon, initial mutable Saga Progression, and reveal-safety validation.

The existing Narrative Planner and long-term arc planning remain responsible for plotlines, episode structure, scenes, pacing, and choosing how an eligible narrative thread is dramatized. Phase 4 does not duplicate those responsibilities.

## Canon and progression

Saga Canon contains the central question, deep truth, durable desire, fundamental fear, stakes, hidden forces, possible transformations, reveal layers, and forbidden early reveals. Saga Progression contains character-known facts, current beliefs, revealed clues, false leads, unresolved questions, and the current reveal stage.

The required invariant is `deepTruth != characterKnowledge != currentBelief`. Story activity may change progression but must not casually rewrite protected Canon.

## Story-context boundary

Ordinary story generation receives a safe Saga projection: central question, durable desire, stakes, known facts, beliefs, eligible clues, unresolved questions, and reveal stage. Hidden deep truth, forbidden early reveals, and future reveal-layer content are excluded.

Later Saga progression work may advance reveal eligibility using story history, relationships, and world state. Episode count by itself is not a reveal rule.

## Phase boundary

Onboarding commit, Living World Bootstrap, NPC/event/rumor materialization, and New Adventure opportunities remain responsibilities of later Genesis phases.
