# Safety, Ethics and Child Wellbeing

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Every design decision in LUMI must prioritize the child's emotional wellbeing.

Safety is not a separate feature.

It is a foundation shared by every system.

---

# Philosophy

Children should feel:

- safe
- respected
- understood
- curious
- hopeful

The application should never intentionally create emotional harm.

---

# Emotional Safety

Stories may contain:

- uncertainty
- disappointment
- temporary sadness
- gentle fear

However, every difficult emotion should eventually move toward understanding, healing or hope.

---

# No Manipulation

The application must never encourage:

- addiction
- fear of missing out
- artificial urgency
- compulsive rewards
- endless engagement loops

Children should always feel comfortable stopping and returning later.

---

# Respect for Choice

Children are never punished for exploring differently.

Ignoring a story is a valid choice.

Choosing another path is a valid choice.

The world adapts naturally.

---

# Privacy

Children's information should remain protected.

Only the minimum information required for the experience should be stored.

Parents remain in control of family data.

---

# Ethical AI

Artificial Intelligence exists to support storytelling.

It must never replace parental guidance.

It must never manipulate emotions for engagement.

It should encourage empathy, curiosity and creativity.

---

# Design Goal

Create an emotionally safe universe where trust is more important than engagement.
