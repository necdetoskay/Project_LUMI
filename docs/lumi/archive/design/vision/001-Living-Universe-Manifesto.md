# Living Universe Manifesto

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Manifesto

LUMI is built upon a simple belief:

A world should exist independently from the child who visits it.

The child is not the reason the world exists.

The child is simply another life within it.

---

# A Living World

When the application is closed, the universe does not disappear.

Villages continue their routines.

Animals continue migrating.

Rain continues falling.

Families continue growing.

Festivals arrive.

People grow older.

Nothing pauses simply because the child is away.

---

# Stories Are Not Content

Traditional games create content.

LUMI creates life.

Stories are only moments where life becomes visible.

A story is never written first.

Life happens first.

---

# No Quest Givers

Nobody waits forever with an exclamation mark above their head.

If someone needed help yesterday, another villager may already have helped today.

The world moves forward.

---

# Every Character Has a Life

Every important character has:

- routines
- dreams
- fears
- memories
- relationships
- responsibilities

The child may become part of those lives, but never owns them.

---

# The World Remembers

Every meaningful action leaves traces.

Small actions may become important years later.

Nothing meaningful is completely forgotten.

---

# Slow Change

The universe changes gradually.

Forests grow.

Buildings decay.

Friendships deepen.

Cultures evolve.

History accumulates.

Children experience a believable passage of time rather than sudden transformations.

---

# Emotional Truth

LUMI values emotional consistency over dramatic twists.

Characters react according to who they are.

Events have understandable consequences.

Kindness builds trust.

Neglect creates distance.

Forgiveness takes time.

---

# Wonder Through Continuity

Wonder is created when the child realizes:

"The world remembered."

Not because something impossible happened, but because something meaningful continued.
