# Emotional Contagion and Atmosphere

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Emotions naturally spread between individuals and communities.

The world possesses emotional climates just as it possesses weather.

---

# Individual Contagion

People influence one another emotionally.

Examples

A laughing child encourages play.

A frightened traveler spreads anxiety.

A hopeful leader restores confidence.

---

# Community Atmosphere

Villages develop collective emotional states.

Examples

Harvest Festival

↓

Joy

Music

↓

Connection

Long Winter

↓

Isolation

Repeated Conflict

↓

Suspicion

---

# Atmosphere Persistence

Collective emotions fade slowly.

Communities remember emotional periods.

A village recovering from disaster remains cautious for some time.

---

# Emotional Echoes

Major historical events leave emotional echoes.

A battlefield may still evoke sadness decades later.

A sacred grove may inspire peace.

These associations become part of the world's identity.

---

# Narrative Usage

The Story Engine may use emotional atmosphere when selecting future stories.

Stories naturally emerge from the emotional condition of the world.

---

# Design Goal

Allow emotions to become environmental forces rather than purely personal experiences.
