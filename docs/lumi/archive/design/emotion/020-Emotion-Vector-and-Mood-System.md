# Emotion Vector and Mood System

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Emotions are represented as multidimensional vectors rather than isolated values.

A character never experiences only one emotion.

Instead, multiple emotional states coexist, interact and continuously influence each other.

---

# Emotion Vector

Examples of dimensions:

- Joy
- Fear
- Curiosity
- Hope
- Trust
- Sadness
- Anger
- Compassion
- Wonder
- Loneliness

Each dimension changes independently.

---

# Emotional Blending

Several emotions may exist simultaneously.

Example

A child entering an ancient cave may feel:

Curiosity

+

Fear

+

Wonder

These combine into a unique emotional experience.

---

# Mood

Mood represents long-term emotional climate.

Unlike emotions, mood changes slowly.

Examples

Optimistic

Calm

Anxious

Melancholic

Hopeful

Mood influences how future emotions are interpreted.

---

# Emotional Momentum

Strong emotions rarely disappear instantly.

They gradually lose intensity unless reinforced.

Repeated experiences strengthen emotional momentum.

---

# Personality Interaction

Different personalities react differently.

The same sadness may last:

Hours

Days

Weeks

depending on the individual.

---

# Design Goal

Represent emotional life as a continuous landscape instead of isolated reactions.
