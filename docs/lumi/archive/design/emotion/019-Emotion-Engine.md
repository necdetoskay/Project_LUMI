# Emotion Engine

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The Emotion Engine models how characters experience, regulate and express emotions.

Emotions are not decorative.

They actively influence every important system.

---

# Core Principle

Every emotion has:

A cause.

An intensity.

A duration.

A direction.

A consequence.

---

# Emotional Dimensions

Examples include:

- Joy
- Sadness
- Fear
- Anger
- Curiosity
- Hope
- Shame
- Pride
- Gratitude
- Loneliness
- Wonder
- Calm

Multiple emotions may coexist.

---

# Emotional Triggers

Emotions arise from:

- world events
- memories
- relationships
- physical condition
- success
- failure
- discovery
- danger
- social interactions

---

# Emotional Persistence

Strong emotions gradually fade.

They do not disappear instantly.

Some emotional traces remain within memories for years.

---

# Emotional Influence

Emotions affect:

- decisions
- learning
- curiosity
- exploration
- relationships
- communication
- memory formation
- story opportunities

---

# Emotional Regulation

Different personalities regulate emotions differently.

Some recover quickly.

Others carry emotional weight for long periods.

Supportive relationships may accelerate healing.

---

# Collective Emotion

Communities also possess emotional atmospheres.

Examples

Festival

↓

Village optimism increases.

Disaster

↓

Fear spreads.

Long drought

↓

Hope slowly decreases.

These collective emotions influence individual behavior.

---

# Design Goal

Emotion should become one of the primary driving forces behind the Living Universe rather than a simple character attribute.
