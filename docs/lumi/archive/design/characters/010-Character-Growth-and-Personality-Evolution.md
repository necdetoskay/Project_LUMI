# Character Growth and Personality Evolution

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Characters evolve throughout their lives.

Growth is continuous and experience-driven.

No experience is completely without influence.

---

# Growth Sources

Character development may result from:

- friendships
- failures
- discoveries
- loss
- teaching
- travel
- parenthood
- responsibility
- repeated success

---

# Personality River

Personality evolves like a river.

Small events create tiny changes.

Repeated experiences slowly redirect the overall flow.

This creates believable long-term development.

---

# Growth Speed

Growth should be slow.

One heroic act does not permanently transform someone.

Dozens of meaningful experiences may.

---

# Positive Development

Examples include:

A fearful child gradually becomes confident.

A lonely traveler slowly learns trust.

A stubborn villager becomes more open-minded.

---

# Negative Development

Growth is not always positive.

Repeated betrayal may reduce trust.

Constant failure may reduce confidence.

Isolation may weaken empathy.

---

# Recovery

Negative experiences are not permanent.

Supportive relationships may gradually heal emotional wounds.

---

# Design Goal

Allow characters to become different people over long periods while remaining believable.
