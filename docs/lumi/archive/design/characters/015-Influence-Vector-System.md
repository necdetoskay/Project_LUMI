# Influence Vector System

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Influence in LUMI is never represented by a single number.

Every action affects different people in different ways.

Therefore influence is modeled as multidimensional vectors.

---

# Core Philosophy

The same event may produce entirely different effects depending on:

- personality
- memories
- fears
- culture
- relationships
- current emotions

Example:

A solar eclipse.

One child becomes fascinated.

Another becomes frightened.

An astronomer celebrates.

An elder interprets it as an ancient sign.

The event is identical.

Its influence is not.

---

# Vector Categories

Possible influence dimensions include:

- Emotional
- Social
- Cultural
- Educational
- Political
- Environmental
- Familial
- Spiritual
- Historical

Each dimension evolves independently.

---

# Propagation

Influence spreads through networks.

Character

↓

Family

↓

Friends

↓

Village

↓

Region

↓

Culture

Small actions may gradually reshape entire communities.

---

# Time

Influence changes over time.

Immediate effects may disappear.

Long-term effects may strengthen.

Some influences remain dormant for years before becoming relevant again.

---

# Narrative Usage

The Story Engine evaluates influence vectors when determining which consequences deserve narrative attention.

Large influence does not always mean dramatic events.

Sometimes the most meaningful influence is quiet and deeply personal.

---

# Design Goal

Replace simple reputation scores with rich, interconnected consequences that ripple naturally through the living universe.
