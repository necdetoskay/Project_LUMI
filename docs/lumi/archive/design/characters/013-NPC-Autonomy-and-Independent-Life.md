# NPC Autonomy and Independent Life

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

NPCs live independently of the child's actions.

They possess agency.

The world belongs to everyone, not only the player.

---

# Daily Life

Every NPC has routines.

Examples:

- working
- studying
- traveling
- cooking
- resting
- visiting friends
- caring for animals
- attending celebrations

---

# Independent Decisions

NPCs solve problems themselves whenever possible.

A broken bridge may be repaired before the child ever arrives.

Lost animals may already have been rescued.

Arguments may already have been resolved.

The world never waits.

---

# Autonomous Relationships

Characters create friendships.

Arguments.

Families.

Communities.

Mentorships.

These evolve without player intervention.

---

# Personal Projects

NPCs may spend weeks pursuing long-term ambitions.

Examples:

Building a windmill.

Learning music.

Writing stories.

Training animals.

Studying medicine.

---

# Unexpected Outcomes

Because NPCs are autonomous, surprising situations naturally emerge.

A child may revisit a village months later and discover:

- new families
- abandoned homes
- rebuilt bridges
- growing forests
- retired elders
- new leaders

None of these require handcrafted scripting.

---

# Design Goal

Create the illusion that every character has a life beyond the visible story.
