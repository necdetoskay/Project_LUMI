# Character Foundation

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Every living being in LUMI is represented as a character.

A character is not merely a collection of attributes.

A character possesses identity, history, relationships, emotions, memories and goals.

The simulation always treats characters as living individuals rather than gameplay objects.

---

# Identity

Every character has a unique identity.

Identity consists of:

- Name
- Age
- Species
- Birthplace
- Family
- Occupation
- Current Residence
- Culture
- Personality
- Life History

Identity persists throughout the character's lifetime.

---

# Character Layers

Each character contains multiple interconnected layers.

Physical Layer

Body, health, appearance and biological needs.

Psychological Layer

Personality, emotions, fears, dreams and motivations.

Social Layer

Family, friendships, reputation and social roles.

Historical Layer

Past experiences and important life events.

Narrative Layer

The stories currently affecting the character.

---

# Character Goals

Characters continuously pursue goals.

Examples include:

- finding food
- protecting children
- repairing a house
- becoming a teacher
- exploring unknown places
- helping friends

Goals arise naturally from needs and circumstances.

---

# Character Consistency

Characters should behave consistently.

A timid child should not suddenly become fearless without meaningful experiences.

Likewise, a generous villager should not become selfish overnight.

Gradual evolution is preferred over abrupt change.

---

# Living Identity

Identity is never frozen.

People learn.

Forget.

Change professions.

Move to new places.

Form families.

Lose friends.

Everything contributes to an evolving life story.

---

# Design Goal

Create believable individuals rather than functional NPCs.
