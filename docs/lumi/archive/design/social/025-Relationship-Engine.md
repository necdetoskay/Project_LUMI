# Relationship Engine

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Relationships are living systems.

Every interaction slightly changes how characters perceive one another.

Relationships evolve continuously rather than switching between predefined states.

---

# Relationship Dimensions

Relationships are represented using multiple vectors.

Examples

Trust

Respect

Affection

Gratitude

Fear

Curiosity

Responsibility

Admiration

Dependence

Forgiveness

Each dimension evolves independently.

---

# Relationship Formation

Relationships begin with small interactions.

Examples

Helping someone.

Sharing food.

Learning together.

Traveling together.

Keeping promises.

---

# Relationship Evolution

Relationships strengthen through repetition.

One kind action creates interest.

Many kind actions create trust.

Years of trust create friendship.

---

# Relationship Damage

Relationships may weaken through:

Broken promises

Neglect

Dishonesty

Repeated conflict

Distance

Fear

Decay is gradual unless caused by major events.

---

# Recovery

Damaged relationships can heal.

Healing requires:

time

communication

shared experiences

consistent actions

Trust is easier to lose than regain.

---

# Narrative Importance

Relationships are one of the strongest sources of emergent stories.

Many future stories originate from previous relationships.

---

# Design Goal

Relationships should feel alive, fragile and meaningful.
