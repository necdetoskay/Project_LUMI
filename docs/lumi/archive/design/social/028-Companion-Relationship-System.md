# Companion Relationship System

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Some relationships naturally become companionships.

Companions are not permanent party members.

They are individuals whose journeys temporarily intersect.

---

# Philosophy

A companion joins because they choose to.

Not because the player recruited them.

Every companion has personal reasons for traveling together.

---

# Companion Formation

Companionship may emerge through:

- shared goals
- mutual trust
- gratitude
- curiosity
- protection
- friendship
- family bonds

No predefined recruitment system exists.

---

# Shared Journey

While traveling together, companions naturally:

- converse
- help each other
- disagree
- teach
- learn
- create memories

These experiences strengthen or weaken the relationship.

---

# Leaving

Companions eventually leave.

Reasons include:

- family responsibilities
- personal ambitions
- danger
- completed objectives
- changing priorities

Departure is a natural part of life.

---

# Reunion

Former companions may return years later.

Their shared memories influence future interactions.

No relationship truly resets.

---

# Narrative Role

Companions allow stories to become emotionally richer without forcing permanent teams.

---

# Design Goal

Create meaningful temporary companionships that feel like real friendships rather than gameplay mechanics.
