# Language, Communication and Dialogue

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Dialogue reflects personality, culture, age and emotional state.

Characters should never sound identical.

---

# Individual Voice

Each character develops a recognizable communication style.

Examples

Short sentences.

Long explanations.

Gentle humor.

Quiet observations.

Curious questions.

---

# Emotional Speech

Emotions naturally affect communication.

Fear creates hesitation.

Joy increases enthusiasm.

Sadness reduces expression.

Wonder encourages questions.

---

# Silence

Silence is meaningful.

Characters sometimes communicate by:

waiting

watching

smiling

remaining quiet

Silence may carry stronger emotional impact than words.

---

# Cultural Influence

Language reflects local traditions.

Different regions develop unique expressions, greetings and storytelling styles.

---

# Child-Friendly Communication

Dialogue should remain:

clear

warm

natural

emotionally understandable

Age-appropriate language always has priority over complexity.

---

# Design Goal

Dialogue should reveal character rather than merely deliver information.
