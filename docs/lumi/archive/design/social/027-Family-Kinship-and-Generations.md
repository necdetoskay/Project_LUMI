# Family, Kinship and Generations

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Families are one of the most important long-term systems in LUMI.

They provide continuity across generations.

---

# Family Structure

A family is more than biological relationships.

Families may include:

Parents

Children

Grandparents

Guardians

Adopted members

Chosen family

Mentors

---

# Living Family Graph

Family relationships are represented as a living graph.

Connections evolve through:

care

responsibility

shared memories

conflict

reconciliation

time

---

# Generational Change

Children grow.

Parents age.

Grandparents pass away.

New generations appear.

Family history continuously expands.

---

# Family Traditions

Families create unique customs.

Examples

Special meals.

Songs.

Stories.

Annual celebrations.

Shared values.

These traditions strengthen identity.

---

# Family Influence

Family strongly affects:

Personality

Language

Beliefs

Skills

Emotional resilience

Social behavior

---

# Legacy

Every family leaves traces.

Examples

A famous craft.

A library.

A lighthouse.

A garden planted generations ago.

Legacy survives individuals.

---

# Narrative Role

Many emotional stories originate within families.

Family history provides endless opportunities for meaningful storytelling.

---

# Design Goal

Families should become living historical organisms rather than static relationship trees.
