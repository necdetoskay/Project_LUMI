# Social Graph and Community Dynamics

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Every character belongs to one or more social networks.

These networks continuously evolve.

The Social Graph represents how individuals, families and communities are connected.

---

# Connections

Connections include:

Friendship

Family

Mentorship

Trade

Cooperation

Rivalry

Leadership

Shared history

Shared interests

---

# Community Behavior

Communities develop behaviors that cannot be explained by any single individual.

Examples

Villages become welcoming.

Communities become cautious.

Families become influential.

Markets become prosperous.

---

# Information Flow

Information spreads through social connections.

Close relationships exchange information quickly.

Distant communities receive slower and less accurate information.

Rumors naturally emerge.

---

# Social Influence

Highly connected individuals influence others more strongly.

Teachers.

Village elders.

Merchants.

Storytellers.

Parents.

Influence is earned rather than assigned.

---

# Dynamic Communities

Communities never remain static.

Members arrive.

Families leave.

Children grow.

Leaders retire.

New traditions emerge.

---

# Design Goal

Represent society as a living network instead of isolated NPCs.
