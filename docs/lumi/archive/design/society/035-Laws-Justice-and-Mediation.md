# Laws, Justice and Mediation

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Every community develops rules that reflect its values.

Justice aims to restore balance rather than simply punish wrongdoing.

---

# Local Laws

Each settlement may define rules regarding:

- shared property
- forests
- animals
- water
- trade
- family responsibilities
- festivals

Rules emerge from local culture.

---

# Conflict Resolution

Disagreements may be resolved through:

- conversation
- mediation
- community meetings
- restitution
- forgiveness

Punishment is only one possible outcome.

---

# Evidence

Communities seek understanding.

Witnesses.

Physical evidence.

Personal reputation.

Past behavior.

All may contribute to resolving disputes.

---

# Mercy

Justice includes compassion.

Communities sometimes value forgiveness over punishment.

Repeated harmful behavior, however, gradually reduces opportunities for mercy.

---

# Long-Term Consequences

Every decision influences future trust.

Fair justice strengthens society.

Unfair justice weakens it.

---

# Narrative Usage

Many meaningful stories emerge from misunderstandings rather than villains.

The Justice System encourages empathy, dialogue and reconciliation.

---

# Design Goal

Justice should strengthen communities while teaching responsibility and understanding.
