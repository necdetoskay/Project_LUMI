# Politics, Governance and Society

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Communities organize themselves in different ways.

Government emerges naturally from culture, history and local needs.

---

# Governance Models

Examples include:

- village councils
- elders
- elected leaders
- family leadership
- cooperative communities
- guild organizations

Different regions may use different systems.

---

# Leadership

Leaders possess influence rather than absolute authority.

Good leadership depends on:

- trust
- competence
- fairness
- experience
- community support

Leadership may change peacefully over time.

---

# Community Decisions

Communities solve issues collectively.

Examples

Building bridges.

Planning festivals.

Protecting forests.

Managing shared resources.

---

# Social Stability

Stability depends on:

- prosperity
- trust
- justice
- cooperation
- environmental conditions

Instability creates new story opportunities.

---

# Politics Without Conflict

Politics is not primarily about power struggles.

Its purpose is to coordinate community life.

Most political decisions are ordinary rather than dramatic.

---

# Design Goal

Governance should feel like an everyday part of society instead of a source of constant conflict.
