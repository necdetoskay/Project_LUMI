# Historical Interpretation and Legend Evolution

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Facts slowly transform into stories.

Stories slowly transform into legends.

Legends gradually shape culture.

---

# Interpretation

Every retelling changes something.

Small details disappear.

Symbolic meaning grows.

Characters become larger than life.

---

# Legend Formation

A simple event may evolve.

Example

A child once rescued a wounded fox.

Years later:

"The Forest Guardian once protected every lost child."

The emotional truth survives even if factual details change.

---

# Symbol Creation

Objects and places may gain symbolic meaning.

Examples

- an old tree
- a bridge
- a lighthouse
- a cave
- a song

Symbols strengthen cultural identity.

---

# Myth and Reality

Legends are not always historically accurate.

They express collective values more than objective facts.

---

# Narrative Usage

The Story Engine may connect present-day events with old legends.

Children gradually discover that legends often began with ordinary lives.

---

# Design Goal

Allow history to become emotionally meaningful through storytelling across generations.
