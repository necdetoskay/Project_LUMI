# Culture and Identity System

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Culture gives every region its unique identity.

It emerges naturally through history, geography and shared experiences.

---

# Components

Culture includes:

- traditions
- beliefs
- celebrations
- clothing
- architecture
- music
- food
- symbols
- stories
- social customs

---

# Cultural Evolution

Cultures never remain frozen.

Generations gradually introduce change.

Outside influences reshape traditions.

Major historical events create new customs.

---

# Local Identity

Each settlement develops its own personality.

Examples

Mountain villages value resilience.

Coastal towns celebrate the sea.

Forest communities respect nature.

Identity emerges from lived experience.

---

# Festivals

Festivals preserve collective memory.

They strengthen community bonds.

They introduce children to history without formal teaching.

---

# Shared Values

Cultures influence:

hospitality

cooperation

respect

courage

curiosity

responsibility

Different communities emphasize different values.

---

# Cultural Exchange

Travel encourages the exchange of ideas.

Music.

Recipes.

Crafts.

Stories.

Beliefs.

Knowledge spreads through people rather than systems.

---

# Narrative Importance

Culture continuously generates story opportunities.

Traditions create celebrations.

Celebrations create meetings.

Meetings create relationships.

Relationships create stories.

---

# Design Goal

Every region should feel like home to someone rather than simply another location.
