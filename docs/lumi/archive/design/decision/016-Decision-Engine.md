# Decision Engine

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The Decision Engine determines what every living character chooses to do.

Its purpose is not to produce optimal decisions.

Its purpose is to produce believable decisions.

Different characters facing the same situation should often make different choices.

---

# Philosophy

Characters do not calculate the "correct" answer.

They evaluate the world through:

- personality
- memories
- emotions
- current needs
- relationships
- culture
- knowledge
- available opportunities

The final decision is therefore unique to the individual.

---

# Inputs

A decision may consider:

- Current emotions
- Personality vectors
- Physical condition
- Nearby characters
- Active world events
- Personal memories
- Relationships
- Inventory
- Time of day
- Weather
- Culture
- Long-term goals

No single factor dominates every decision.

---

# Multiple Valid Choices

Several actions may be equally reasonable.

Example

A frightened child may:

- hide
- run home
- ask for help
- investigate carefully

Each choice is believable depending on context.

---

# Emergent Decisions

The engine should produce unexpected but understandable behavior.

Characters should occasionally surprise the player while remaining internally consistent.

---

# Learning

After every important decision:

- memories are updated
- confidence changes
- relationships evolve
- personality may slowly adapt

Every decision slightly changes future decisions.

---

# Design Goal

Characters should feel like thinking individuals rather than scripted actors.
