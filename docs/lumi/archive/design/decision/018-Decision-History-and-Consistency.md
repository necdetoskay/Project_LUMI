# Decision History and Consistency

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Past decisions shape future behavior.

Characters develop behavioral consistency over time.

---

# Philosophy

Identity is built through repeated choices.

One courageous action does not create a brave person.

Years of courageous actions may.

---

# Decision History

Important choices are remembered.

Examples

Helped strangers.

Ignored someone in need.

Protected family.

Kept promises.

Broke promises.

Showed mercy.

Acted selfishly.

---

# Behavioral Tendencies

Repeated patterns gradually influence:

- courage
- generosity
- patience
- honesty
- confidence
- responsibility

These changes are intentionally slow.

---

# Context Matters

Consistency never becomes rigidity.

Even a generous person may refuse help when exhausted.

Even a fearful person may perform heroic acts under extraordinary circumstances.

---

# Reputation

Decision history affects:

Personal identity.

Relationships.

Community perception.

Historical legacy.

---

# Reflection

Some characters naturally reflect on previous decisions.

Reflection may strengthen learning.

Wise characters often learn faster from experience.

---

# Design Goal

Create believable long-term behavioral evolution through accumulated decisions.
