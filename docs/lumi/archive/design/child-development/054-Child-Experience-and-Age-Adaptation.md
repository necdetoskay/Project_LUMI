Version: Draft v1.0

---

# Purpose

Every child experiences the Living Universe differently.

The world adapts to developmental needs while preserving its identity.

---

# Age Adaptation

Storytelling adjusts according to age.

Younger children receive:

- shorter stories
- simpler dialogue
- clear emotional signals
- gentle pacing

Older children gradually encounter:

- more complex relationships
- deeper choices
- longer narrative arcs
- richer world history

---

# Emotional Safety

Stories should never overwhelm.

Fear creates tension.

Hope restores balance.

Children always retain emotional security.

---

# Encouraging Curiosity

Questions are more valuable than answers.

The world intentionally leaves space for imagination.

Not every mystery is solved immediately.

---

# Positive Agency

Children should feel capable of influencing the world.

Even small actions may eventually matter.

---

# Growing With The Child

As children mature, the universe slowly reveals greater depth.

LUMI grows alongside its audience.

---

# Design Goal

Provide emotionally safe experiences that remain meaningful across different ages.
