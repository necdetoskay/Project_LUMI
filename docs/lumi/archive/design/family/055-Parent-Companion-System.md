# Parent Companion System

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Parents are companions in the child's journey.

The system extends meaningful moments beyond the screen.

---

# Philosophy

LUMI is designed to strengthen family conversations.

Parents are invited to participate without interrupting the child's sense of discovery.

---

# Parent Insights

The system may occasionally provide gentle summaries such as:

- important friendships
- emotional milestones
- memorable discoveries
- acts of kindness
- meaningful choices

These summaries support conversation rather than evaluation.

---

# Conversation Starters

Examples

"What did you enjoy most today?"

"Which character surprised you?"

"How do you think the fox felt?"

The goal is reflection, not testing.

---

# Family Memories

Families gradually build a shared archive containing:

- favorite stories
- memorable characters
- important discoveries
- emotional milestones

This archive becomes part of the family's own history.

---

# Real-World Activities

Some stories may inspire optional offline activities.

Examples

- planting a flower
- drawing a favorite scene
- observing the night sky
- listening to birds
- writing a new ending together

The application encourages real-world experiences whenever appropriate.

---

# Design Goal

Transform digital storytelling into meaningful family experiences that continue beyond the application.
