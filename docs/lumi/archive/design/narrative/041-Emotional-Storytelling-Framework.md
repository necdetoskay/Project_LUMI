# Emotional Storytelling Framework

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Stories should primarily create emotional experiences.

Learning emerges naturally from those experiences.

---

# Emotional Journey

Every meaningful story follows an emotional rhythm.

Curiosity

↓

Discovery

↓

Challenge

↓

Reflection

↓

Resolution

↓

Memory

Not every story requires every phase.

---

# Emotional Diversity

The universe should naturally produce stories containing:

- joy
- kindness
- courage
- fear
- sadness
- surprise
- gratitude
- forgiveness
- hope
- wonder

No single emotion should dominate for long periods.

---

# Quiet Moments

Silence is valuable.

Examples

Watching rain.

Walking through a forest.

Listening to birds.

Looking at the stars.

These moments strengthen emotional pacing.

---

# Safe Conflict

Conflict should encourage understanding.

Children should never feel emotionally trapped.

Hope must always remain possible.

---

# Recovery

Every emotionally difficult experience should eventually allow recovery.

Healing is part of the story.

Not merely the ending.

---

# Lasting Memories

The strongest stories are often the quietest ones.

Emotional depth is preferred over dramatic spectacle.

---

# Design Goal

Help children build meaningful emotional memories instead of simply consuming stories.
