# Choice, Consequence and Player Agency

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Children should feel that their choices matter.

Not because the game rewards them.

Because the world responds naturally.

---

# Philosophy

There are rarely correct choices.

There are only different consequences.

Every choice teaches something.

---

# Natural Consequences

Example

The child helps a gardener.

↓

Flowers bloom earlier next spring.

↓

Butterflies return.

↓

Another child discovers the meadow.

The consequence emerges naturally.

---

# No Punishment Design

The system avoids artificial punishment.

Mistakes become learning opportunities.

Even ignored opportunities may create interesting future stories.

---

# Freedom

Children may:

help

observe

leave

return later

ignore

change their minds

The world accepts all of these possibilities.

---

# World Response

The universe reacts rather than judges.

Characters remember.

Relationships evolve.

History changes.

Nothing simply resets.

---

# Long-Term Agency

Small choices may slowly reshape communities.

The child rarely notices immediate impact.

Instead, meaningful change becomes visible over time.

---

# Design Goal

Empower children through meaningful participation rather than through game mechanics.
