# Narrative Consciousness Layer

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The Living Universe constantly produces events.

Not every event deserves to become a story.

The Narrative Consciousness Layer decides what is emotionally meaningful enough to present to the child.

---

# Philosophy

This layer does not create stories.

It notices them.

It behaves like an attentive observer.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Potential stories are evaluated using:

Emotional significance.

Character development.

Relationship impact.

Historical relevance.

Curiosity.

Wonder.

Novelty.

Continuity.

---

# Emotional Rhythm

The system maintains healthy pacing.

Several intense stories should never appear consecutively.

Quiet moments are intentionally preserved.

---

# Hidden Operation

Children never see this layer.

They simply experience a world where meaningful stories appear naturally.

---

# Long-Term Awareness

The layer remembers previous stories.

It avoids repetition.

It reconnects forgotten threads.

It creates satisfying continuity across months or years.

---

# Design Goal

Allow the universe to recognize meaningful stories without ever feeling scripted.
