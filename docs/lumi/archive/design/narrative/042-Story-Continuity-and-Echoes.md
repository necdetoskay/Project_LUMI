# Story Continuity and Echoes

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Stories should continue existing after they end.

Every completed story leaves echoes throughout the Living Universe.

---

# Echoes

Story echoes may appear as:

- changed relationships
- remembered promises
- rebuilt locations
- new traditions
- emotional growth
- community memories
- personal habits

Stories become part of the world's history.

---

# Delayed Consequences

Some consequences appear immediately.

Others emerge months later.

Example

Helping a child today

↓

Years later

↓

That child becomes the village healer.

---

# Returning Characters

Characters naturally remember previous adventures.

Old conversations influence future dialogue.

Forgotten friends may unexpectedly return.

---

# Living Locations

Places remember stories.

An abandoned cabin.

A tree planted together.

A repaired bridge.

A hidden cave.

Locations become emotional landmarks.

---

# Story Chains

One completed story often becomes the seed of another.

Nothing exists in isolation.

---

# Design Goal

Allow children to feel that every meaningful experience permanently enriches the Living Universe.
