# Wildlife, Animal Societies and Nature Intelligence

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Animals are living beings with routines, instincts and memories.

They are not collectible creatures or moving decorations.

---

# Animal Life

Animals:

- search for food
- raise offspring
- migrate
- protect territory
- avoid danger
- explore

Every species behaves differently.

---

# Animal Families

Many species develop family structures.

Examples

Foxes protect cubs.

Birds teach fledglings.

Elephants remember family members.

Wolves cooperate as packs.

Family behavior emerges naturally.

---

# Animal Memory

Animals remember:

safe places

dangerous places

friendly humans

predators

migration paths

Memory influences future behavior.

---

# Nature Intelligence

Animal behavior emerges from:

- instincts
- learning
- environment
- previous experiences

The system does not require human-like reasoning.

---

# Ecosystem Relationships

Predators.

Prey.

Pollinators.

Seed spreaders.

Scavengers.

Every species contributes to ecological balance.

---

# Human Interaction

Relationships between humans and animals evolve naturally.

Examples

Repeated kindness creates trust.

Repeated hunting increases caution.

Animals adapt through experience.

---

# Design Goal

Wildlife should feel alive, intelligent and emotionally believable without becoming human.
