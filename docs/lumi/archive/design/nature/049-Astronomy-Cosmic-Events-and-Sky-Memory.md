# Astronomy, Cosmic Events and Sky Memory

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The sky is a living part of the universe.

Children should naturally develop curiosity about the stars, the moon and the changing heavens.

---

# Celestial Objects

Examples include:

- the Sun
- the Moon
- stars
- planets
- constellations
- meteors
- comets

The sky changes continuously.

---

# Cosmic Events

Rare events may include:

- eclipses
- meteor showers
- unusual moon colors
- bright comets
- seasonal constellations

These events are uncommon and memorable.

---

# Cultural Meaning

Different cultures interpret the same event differently.

A meteor shower may be:

A celebration.

A scientific curiosity.

A spiritual symbol.

A beautiful mystery.

No single interpretation is universally correct.

---

# Sky Memory

Characters remember remarkable celestial events.

Communities may later celebrate them through:

festivals

songs

stories

traditions

The sky becomes part of cultural history.

---

# Wonder

Looking at the night sky should inspire quiet curiosity.

Not every mystery needs an explanation.

Wonder itself has value.

---

# Design Goal

The sky should encourage imagination rather than merely providing background visuals.
