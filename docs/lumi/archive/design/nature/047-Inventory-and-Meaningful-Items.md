# Inventory and Meaningful Items

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Inventory exists to support storytelling.

It is not designed for accumulation or optimization.

---

# Philosophy

Children should keep objects because they matter emotionally.

Not because they increase power.

---

# Meaningful Items

Examples

A feather received from a friend.

A map discovered during exploration.

A handmade flute.

A pressed flower.

A letter.

Each object carries memories.

---

# Story Integration

Items may:

unlock memories

continue stories

trigger conversations

strengthen relationships

preserve family history

Objects naturally participate in narratives.

---

# Personal Collections

Children gradually build collections reflecting their own journeys.

Every inventory becomes unique.

---

# Visual Simplicity

The interface should remain clean.

Focus remains on the meaning of each object rather than statistics.

---

# Permanent Memories

Some objects remain with the child throughout the entire journey.

Years later they may still trigger old memories and forgotten stories.

---

# Design Goal

Inventory should become a memory album instead of a storage system.
