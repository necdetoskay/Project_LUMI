# Weather, Seasons and Environmental Effects

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Weather is not a visual effect.

It is a living environmental system that influences every aspect of the world.

---

# Dynamic Weather

Weather continuously evolves.

Examples include:

- sunshine
- rain
- snowfall
- storms
- fog
- strong winds
- heat waves

Weather changes naturally over time.

---

# Environmental Influence

Weather affects:

- travel
- wildlife
- farming
- rivers
- festivals
- emotions
- visibility
- story opportunities

No weather exists without consequences.

---

# Seasonal Cycle

The world progresses through seasons.

Spring

Summer

Autumn

Winter

Each season reshapes the environment.

---

# Emotional Atmosphere

Weather contributes to emotional storytelling.

Examples

Gentle rain encourages reflection.

Snow creates silence.

Morning fog inspires mystery.

A warm sunset creates comfort.

---

# Long-Term Effects

Repeated weather events gradually reshape the world.

Floods alter rivers.

Snow damages roads.

Drought changes vegetation.

Nature remembers climate.

---

# Narrative Usage

The Story Engine uses weather as a natural storytelling element.

Weather never appears only for dramatic effect.

It exists because the world is alive.

---

# Design Goal

Children should feel that weather belongs to the world rather than being generated for them.
