# World Simulation Engine

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The World Simulation Engine is the heart of Project LUMI.

Its responsibility is to ensure that the universe continues living independently of the child's presence.

It continuously updates the world using the current state of every interconnected system.

Stories are never simulated directly.

Life is simulated.

Stories emerge naturally.

---

# Responsibilities

The engine is responsible for:

- advancing time
- updating settlements
- simulating NPC routines
- updating ecology
- processing weather
- evolving cultures
- resolving ongoing events
- generating new opportunities
- maintaining world consistency

---

# Simulation Philosophy

The simulation should never attempt to calculate everything.

Only meaningful parts of the world receive detailed simulation.

The farther something is from the child's current narrative relevance, the more abstract the simulation becomes.

This keeps the world believable while remaining computationally efficient.

---

# Layers of Simulation

Layer 1

Immediate surroundings.

High detail.

Every NPC action may be simulated.

---

Layer 2

Nearby regions.

Medium detail.

Important events continue individually.

Routine behavior is aggregated.

---

Layer 3

Distant world.

Low detail.

Only major historical changes are processed.

---

# Event Propagation

Actions ripple outward.

Example:

A bridge collapses.

↓

Travel becomes slower.

↓

Trade decreases.

↓

Food prices increase.

↓

Village mood changes.

↓

New story opportunities emerge.

---

# Deterministic Behavior

Given identical inputs, the simulation should produce identical outcomes.

Randomness only exists where uncertainty naturally belongs.

---

# Simulation Tick

The engine progresses through simulation ticks.

Each tick may process:

- world updates
- NPC updates
- ecology updates
- event resolution
- relationship changes
- historical recording

---

# Goal

Create the illusion of a world that never stops living.
