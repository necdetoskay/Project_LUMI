# Objects, Items and World Persistence

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Objects persist within the Living Universe.

Every meaningful object possesses history.

Objects are remembered.

Moved.

Repaired.

Inherited.

Lost.

Rediscovered.

---

# Object Identity

Objects may contain:

Origin

Creator

Current Owner

Previous Owners

Condition

Age

Location

History

Emotional Value

---

# Aging

Objects naturally change.

Wood weathers.

Metal rusts.

Books fade.

Homes require repair.

Time leaves visible traces.

---

# Ownership

Objects may change hands.

Through:

gifts

inheritance

trade

discovery

loss

Each transfer becomes part of the object's story.

---

# Emotional Objects

Certain items become emotionally meaningful.

Examples

A child's drawing.

A family necklace.

An old lantern.

A wooden toy.

Their importance comes from memories rather than rarity.

---

# Narrative Role

Objects frequently reconnect stories.

A forgotten key may reopen an old chapter years later.

---

# Design Goal

Objects should become witnesses of history rather than inventory entries.
