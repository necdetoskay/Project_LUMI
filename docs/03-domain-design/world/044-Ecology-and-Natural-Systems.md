# Ecology and Natural Systems

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Nature is a living system.

Plants, rivers, mountains, forests and seasons constantly interact with one another.

Ecology is not decoration.

It is one of the primary engines that shapes the Living Universe.

---

# Living Ecosystems

Every ecosystem contains:

- Flora
- Fauna
- Water
- Climate
- Soil
- Natural Resources

Each component influences the others.

---

# Dynamic Balance

Nature continuously seeks balance.

Examples

Long drought

↓

River level decreases

↓

Fish population declines

↓

Bird migration changes

↓

Villagers catch fewer fish

↓

Local economy changes

One natural event may influence dozens of systems.

---

# Regeneration

Nature slowly heals.

Burned forests regrow.

Rivers recover.

Animals return.

Recovery may take months or years.

---

# Human Influence

Communities affect ecosystems.

Responsible behavior strengthens nature.

Overuse weakens it.

Nature always responds over time.

---

# Seasonal Cycles

Seasons influence:

- plants
- wildlife
- weather
- travel
- agriculture
- festivals

Nothing in nature remains constant.

---

# Design Goal

Nature should feel like an independent living organism rather than scenery.
