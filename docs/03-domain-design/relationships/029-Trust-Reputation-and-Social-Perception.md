# Trust, Reputation and Social Perception

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Trust, reputation and public perception are different concepts.

LUMI models each independently.

---

# Trust

Trust exists between individuals.

It grows through:

- honesty
- reliability
- kindness
- consistency
- shared experiences

Trust is personal.

---

# Reputation

Reputation represents what society believes.

It spreads through:

- stories
- rumors
- witnesses
- public actions

Reputation may differ from reality.

---

# Social Perception

Different communities perceive the same person differently.

Example

A traveler may be:

Trusted by one village.

Feared by another.

Unknown elsewhere.

---

# Rumors

Information changes while spreading.

Each retelling may:

omit details

add assumptions

change emotional tone

Rumors become part of the living world.

---

# Redemption

Reputation is not permanent.

Consistent actions gradually reshape public opinion.

Recovery requires patience.

---

# Design Goal

Separate private relationships from public identity to create believable societies.
