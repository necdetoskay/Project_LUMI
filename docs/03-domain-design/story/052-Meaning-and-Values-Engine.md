# Meaning and Values Engine

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The Meaning and Values Engine connects experiences to personal growth.

LUMI does not teach values through lectures.

Instead, values emerge naturally through lived experiences.

Children discover meaning rather than being instructed.

---

# Philosophy

Values are not objectives.

They are consequences.

Helping another character is valuable because of the relationship it creates, not because it grants a reward.

---

# Core Values

The Living Universe encourages experiences related to:

- kindness
- empathy
- honesty
- curiosity
- responsibility
- courage
- patience
- gratitude
- forgiveness
- cooperation
- hope

None of these values are presented as mandatory lessons.

---

# Meaning Through Experience

Children understand values by seeing their consequences.

Example

Sharing food

↓

Friendship grows

↓

Trust develops

↓

Future opportunities emerge

Meaning develops naturally.

---

# Moral Complexity

Life is rarely black and white.

Sometimes two good choices conflict.

Children are encouraged to think rather than search for the "correct" answer.

---

# Lasting Meaning

The strongest lessons are remembered because they were experienced emotionally.

Meaning remains long after the story ends.

---

# Design Goal

Allow values to emerge naturally from the Living Universe instead of presenting explicit moral instruction.
