# AI Narrative Director

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

The AI Narrative Director is not a story writer.

It is a silent observer and orchestrator.

Its responsibility is to recognize meaningful moments that naturally emerge from the Living Universe and present them to the child at the right time.

The AI never forces stories.

It cultivates them.

---

# Philosophy

Life creates stories.

The AI simply notices them.

Like a gardener, it cannot force a flower to bloom.

It can only create the right conditions.

For this reason the Narrative Director is internally treated as a "Narrative Gardener."

---

# Responsibilities

The Narrative Director continuously observes:

- World State
- Character Goals
- Memories
- Emotions
- Relationships
- World Events
- Weather
- Time
- Culture
- Ecology

It evaluates which combinations possess meaningful narrative potential.

---

# What It Never Does

The AI never creates random drama.

It never invents conflict simply to entertain.

It never breaks character consistency.

It never ignores previous history.

---

# Story Selection

Possible story opportunities are ranked according to:

- Emotional impact
- Character growth
- Curiosity
- Wonder
- Continuity
- Relationship depth
- Historical relevance
- Player fatigue
- Narrative pacing

The highest score is not always selected.

Sometimes a small quiet story is emotionally more valuable.

---

# Narrative Balance

The Director intentionally balances:

Adventure

Reflection

Humor

Wonder

Learning

Sadness

Hope

Recovery

Children should experience emotional variety rather than constant excitement.

---

# Invisible Operation

The child never realizes that stories are being selected.

The world simply feels alive.

---

# Long-Term Planning

The Director may postpone a promising story if another emotional thread deserves attention first.

Stories are timed, not scheduled.

---

# Design Goal

Become an invisible storyteller whose greatest success is remaining unnoticed.
