# Wonder Engine

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Wonder is one of the primary emotions that defines Project LUMI.

The goal is not constant surprise.

The goal is lasting curiosity.

---

# Philosophy

Children should frequently think:

"I wonder what will happen..."

instead of

"I know what comes next."

Wonder grows from discovery.

---

# Sources of Wonder

Wonder may emerge through:

- exploration
- unexpected kindness
- nature
- astronomy
- forgotten places
- old memories
- rare animals
- cultural traditions
- quiet moments

Wonder is often subtle.

---

# Rare Moments

Some experiences should remain uncommon.

Examples

A century-old tree blooms.

A fox remembers a child.

A hidden valley appears after heavy fog.

A forgotten melody is heard again.

Their rarity preserves emotional value.

---

# Curiosity Loop

Wonder naturally creates curiosity.

Curiosity encourages exploration.

Exploration creates memories.

Memories increase emotional attachment.

Attachment creates new wonder.

The cycle continues throughout the child's journey.

---

# No Artificial Rewards

Wonder never exists to unlock achievements.

Its reward is the experience itself.

---

# Design Goal

Create a universe where curiosity becomes stronger than completion.
