# Story Seeds and Narrative Opportunities

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Stories begin as opportunities.

The Story Engine continuously searches the Living Universe for situations that could naturally become meaningful narratives.

---

# Story Seeds

A Story Seed is a small situation with narrative potential.

Examples

A lonely child waiting near a river.

A wounded owl hidden in the forest.

A bridge beginning to collapse.

A forgotten birthday.

A merchant arriving late.

A missing book.

None of these are stories yet.

They are possibilities.

---

# Opportunity Detection

The engine evaluates:

Current world state.

Relationships.

Emotions.

Weather.

History.

Character goals.

Recent events.

Location.

Meaningful combinations become Story Seeds.

---

# Multiple Outcomes

Every seed may grow differently.

The child may participate.

Another NPC may solve it.

Time may resolve it naturally.

The opportunity may disappear.

Nothing is guaranteed.

---

# Organic Timing

Stories appear when they feel appropriate.

The engine avoids overwhelming the child.

Quiet periods are valuable.

---

# Design Goal

Generate opportunities instead of scripted quests.
