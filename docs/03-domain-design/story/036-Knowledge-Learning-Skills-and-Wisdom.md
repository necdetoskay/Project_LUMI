# Knowledge, Learning, Skills and Wisdom

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Knowledge in LUMI is acquired through living.

Characters do not simply gain skills.

They learn by observing, practicing, teaching and experiencing.

---

# Learning Sources

Knowledge may come from:

- family
- teachers
- observation
- experimentation
- books
- travel
- mistakes
- conversations

Every character follows a unique learning path.

---

# Skills

Examples include:

- farming
- fishing
- music
- medicine
- navigation
- cooking
- storytelling
- craftsmanship

Skills improve gradually through repeated use.

---

# Wisdom

Wisdom differs from knowledge.

Knowledge answers:

"How?"

Wisdom answers:

"When?"

"And why?"

Older characters often possess wisdom even if they are not the most knowledgeable.

---

# Teaching

Characters naturally teach one another.

Parents teach children.

Masters teach apprentices.

Friends exchange experiences.

Teaching strengthens both teacher and learner.

---

# Forgotten Knowledge

Unused skills slowly weaken.

Frequently practiced skills become increasingly reliable.

Learning never completely stops.

---

# Design Goal

Knowledge should feel alive, shared and continuously evolving.
