# Purpose

Stories behave like living organisms.

They are born.

They grow.

They influence one another.

Eventually they fade while leaving traces behind.

# Living Ecosystem

Stories compete for attention.

Some combine.

Others quietly disappear.

Some unexpectedly reconnect years later.

---

# Story Interactions

Examples

A village festival overlaps with heavy rain.

↓

New friendships emerge.

↓

Those friendships later influence another story.

Every story affects future stories.

---

# Narrative Diversity

The world naturally produces:

Small stories.

Personal stories.

Family stories.

Community stories.

Historical stories.

No artificial balance is required.

---

# Story Density

The engine regulates narrative density.

Children should never feel overwhelmed.

Periods of peaceful exploration are equally important.

---

# Story Echoes

Completed stories continue influencing:

Relationships.

Locations.

Communities.

Objects.

Memories.

Stories never completely disappear.

---

# Design Goal

Treat stories as living ecological systems rather than isolated missions.
