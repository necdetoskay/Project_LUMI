# NPC Life Cycle

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

NPCs experience complete life cycles.

Their lives are not limited to serving the player.

---

# Life Stages

Birth

↓

Childhood

↓

Adolescence

↓

Adulthood

↓

Old Age

↓

Death

---

# Childhood

Children learn primarily through observation.

Family and community strongly influence personality formation.

---

# Adolescence

Identity begins to stabilize.

Curiosity, independence and friendships become increasingly important.

---

# Adulthood

Responsibilities dominate.

Work.

Family.

Community.

Personal ambitions.

---

# Old Age

Older characters become valuable sources of history and wisdom.

Their memories preserve the world's past.

---

# Death

Death removes the individual but not their influence.

Their actions continue through:

- memories
- descendants
- stories
- traditions
- objects
- places

---

# Legacy

Every meaningful life leaves traces.

Examples:

A bridge built decades ago.

A song composed by a grandmother.

A tree planted during youth.

A school founded by a teacher.

These become part of the world's living history.

---

# Design Goal

Every life should matter beyond its duration.
