# NPC Needs, Goals and Motivations

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Every NPC acts because it wants or needs something.

Characters never perform actions simply because a script says so.

Their decisions emerge from changing needs, motivations and circumstances.

---

# Needs

Needs are continuous.

Examples include:

- Hunger
- Rest
- Safety
- Belonging
- Learning
- Achievement
- Curiosity
- Love
- Purpose
- Freedom

Different personalities prioritize these needs differently.

---

# Goals

Goals are temporary expressions of needs.

Examples

Need:
Safety

↓

Goal:
Repair the damaged roof.

---

Need:
Belonging

↓

Goal:
Attend the village festival.

---

Need:
Curiosity

↓

Goal:
Explore the abandoned ruins.

---

# Motivation Sources

Motivation may originate from:

- emotions
- memories
- promises
- relationships
- culture
- traditions
- responsibilities
- personality

Several motivations may exist simultaneously.

---

# Conflicting Motivations

Characters often face internal conflicts.

Example:

A parent wants to protect their child.

At the same time they must travel to obtain medicine.

Neither choice is perfect.

These conflicts generate believable stories.

---

# Dynamic Priorities

Goals continuously change.

A sudden storm may immediately override every previous objective.

---

# Design Goal

NPCs should appear to pursue meaningful lives rather than executing scripted tasks.
