# Dreams, Imagination and Inner World

Version: Draft v1.0

Status: Design Archive

---

# Purpose

Every character possesses an inner world.

Dreams and imagination reveal emotions that ordinary actions cannot express.

---

# Dreams

Dreams are symbolic rather than predictive.

They may reflect:

- hopes
- fears
- memories
- curiosity
- unresolved emotions

Dreams never replace reality.

---

# Inner Garden

Every character carries an invisible emotional landscape.

This "Inner Garden" slowly changes throughout life.

Positive experiences help it flourish.

Painful experiences may leave quiet scars.

Healing gradually restores balance.

---

# Imagination

Children naturally imagine possibilities.

Imagination supports:

- creativity
- exploration
- storytelling
- emotional resilience

The world respects imagination without confusing it with reality.

---

# Reflection

Quiet moments allow characters to think about previous experiences.

Reflection strengthens emotional growth.

---

# Narrative Role

Dreams occasionally provide emotional insight without revealing future events.

Their purpose is understanding, not prediction.

---

# Design Goal

Represent the invisible emotional life of every character with warmth and subtlety.
