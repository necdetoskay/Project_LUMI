## Long-horizon master program

Aynı canonical universe üzerinde 10 -> 25 -> 50 -> 100+ story checkpoints. Master assertions:

- timeline/temporal causality
- world/location persistence
- NPC identity/autonomy
- episodic/semantic memory and retrieval
- growth drift
- relationship continuity
- inventory/object identity and ownership
- Saga continuity/emergence/pacing
- knowledge/rumor/belief/secret boundaries
- environment/weather consequences
- progressive topology consistency
- settlement/culture/governance continuity
- opportunity diversity
- narrative quality/no-gamification leakage
- session/replay/idempotency
- tenant/family/child isolation
- context/token/cost growth

Master acceptance: ayrı motorların PASS olması yeterli değildir; combined living-universe journey içinde birbirlerini bozmadıkları kanıtlanmalıdır.
